# Privacy Policy — highlights.live

*Effective date: public-beta launch. Last updated: September 2026.*

highlights.live ("the Service") is a product operated by **AV LLC** ("we",
"us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect through
highlights.live, how we use it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.
By using the Service during the public beta you agree to this policy along with
our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).

## What we collect

- **Contact / account information.** Your email address when you join the
  waitlist or create an account, and the authentication credentials you set
  (we store passwords only as salted hashes, never in plain text).
- **Video you provide.** The live streams and VODs you connect, upload, or point
  us at, which we process to detect moments and generate clips.
- **Generated clips and metadata.** The highlight clips we create for you, plus
  associated metadata such as event type, timestamps, and the reason captured.
- **Quota / usage information.** How many clips you generate per month (your
  usage against the plan quota), and related feature usage needed to run and
  meter the Service.
- **Technical data.** IP address, browser/device type, and basic request logs,
  used for security, rate limiting, anti-abuse, and keeping the Service stable.

## How we use your data

- To provide and operate the Service: process your video, detect moments,
  generate and deliver clips, display your clips in your feed, and enforce plan
  quotas.
- To manage your account and waitlist: send account- and invite-related notices,
  and (only with your consent) product updates.
- For security and anti-abuse: enforce rate limits and the beta invite gate, and
  protect against unauthorized access or abuse.
- For product improvement: we may use **aggregate, de-identified** statistics
  (for example, clip volumes) to improve the Service. We do not use your uploaded
  video to train third-party models or to improve products outside of providing
  the Service to you.

## Data retention

- **Clips** on the free beta are retained for **30 days**. Source video is
  retained only as long as needed to generate your clips and no longer than
  **30 days**.
- You may request **deletion at any time** through the process in our
  [Data Retention note](./data-retention.md); we honor deletion requests within
  a reasonable time (5 business days).
- Account, email, and quota records are retained for as long as your account
  exists, and may be kept as required to comply with legal obligations.

## Sharing and disclosure

- **We do not sell your personal data.** We do not rent it, and we do not serve
  ads based on it.
- We share data only with **service providers** we use to operate the Service
  (for example, cloud compute/GPU providers that run the detection and clip
  generation, and hosting providers), each bound to use it only to provide the
  Service to us.
- We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or
  the rights of users.

## Your choices

- **Deletion:** request deletion of clips, source video, or your account at
  any time (see [Data Retention](./data-retention.md)).
- **Correction:** update your account email in the console or by contacting us.
- **Communication:** stop marketing emails at any time; we will still send
  essential service/account notices.

## Children

The Service is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect
personal data from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is material, we will update the effective
date and, where practical, notify you. Your continued use of the Service after a
change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
