# Data Retention — highlights.live

*Effective date: public-beta launch. Last updated: September 2026.*

This note describes how long we keep the video and clips you provide through
highlights.live during the public beta. It is part of our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

## Summary

- **Generated clips** are retained for **30 days** on the free beta plan.
- **Source video** you upload or connect (live stream or VOD) is retained only
  for as long as we need it to generate and deliver your clips, and no longer
  than **30 days** on the free beta plan.
- You can request **deletion at any time**, and we will honor it.

## What we retain

| Data | Retention on the free beta |
|------|----------------------------|
| Source video (uploaded / connected live + VOD streams) | Only as long as needed to generate clips; at most 30 days |
| Generated highlight clips + clip metadata | 30 days |
| Your account, email, and usage (quota) records | For as long as your account exists |
| Raw processing frames / intermediate analysis data | Deleted once a clip is generated and delivered |

## Deletion on request

You can request deletion of any uploaded source video or generated clip (or your
entire account) at any time by contacting **support@highlights.live**. We will
process deletion requests within **5 business days** of receiving a verifiable
request that is linked to the account that owns the data.

Deleting a clip removes the stored clip and its associated source/processing
data. Deleting your account removes your account, stored email, and quota
records (subject to any legal retention requirements).

## Persistence of licensed third-party content

We do not resell or distribute your uploaded content. You retain ownership of
the clips and source video you provide (subject to the license you grant us in
the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md), which is limited to running the
service for you).
